YOSHI RIDE v1.7 update steps

1. GitHub Pages
- Upload and overwrite all files in this ZIP to the yoshi-ride repository.
- Commit to main and wait for GitHub Pages deployment.

2. Supabase Edge Function (required in v1.7)
- Open Edge Functions > cycle-route > Code.
- Replace index.ts with: supabase/functions/cycle-route/index.ts
- Deploy function.
- Keep Verify JWT with legacy secret OFF.
- ORS_API_KEY stays unchanged.

3. After deployment
- Reload YOSHI RIDE.
- Console should show YOSHI RIDE v1.7.0.
- Recalculate the route once. Old v1.6 stored routes do not contain turn instructions.
- In RIDE, tap Voice OFF to switch it to Voice ON before START.

Voice guidance triggers at roughly 200 m, 50 m, and immediately before a turn, plus off-route and arrival alerts.
